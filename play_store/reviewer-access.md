# Abaj TV — Google Play reviewer access

## Launch flow
1. Install and open Abaj TV on an Android TV device or emulator.
2. The app opens the Android TV interface and can be controlled with a D-pad remote.
3. If device linking is required, the app displays the Telegram linking flow.
4. Complete linking through the official Abaj TV Telegram bot, then return to the TV app.
5. The app refreshes access automatically after the account/device is linked.

## Navigation
- D-pad: move through filters, channel cards and player controls.
- OK/Enter: open channel or activate focused control.
- Back: close player / return to channel list.
- Favorites can be added or removed from the player and channel list.

## Notes for review
- Google Play build does not install APK updates itself.
- Google Play build does not request camera or microphone permissions.
- No purchase flow is exposed inside the Play Store build.
- Content availability depends on the reviewer account/device access supplied during review.
