Title:
Abaj TV

Short description:
TV on Android TV with search, favorites and fast channel switching.

Full description:
Abaj TV is an Android TV app for convenient viewing of available TV channels.

Features:
• TV-friendly remote-control interface;
• channel search;
• categories and filters;
• favorites;
• recently viewed channels;
• fast channel switching;
• quality selection when multiple stream variants are available;
• Russian, Armenian and English interface;
• TV linking through Telegram.

Channel availability depends on the connected account and available sources. The app does not guarantee availability of any specific channel or stream quality.

Abaj TV is designed for compatible Android TV devices.
