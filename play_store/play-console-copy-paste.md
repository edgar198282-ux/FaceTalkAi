## App access
Restricted functionality: Yes — device/account linking may be required.

Reviewer instructions:
1. Launch Abaj TV on Android TV.
2. If the TV linking screen appears, open the official Telegram bot @FaceTalkID_bot.
3. Send /start and enter the code shown on the TV, or follow the generated Telegram link.
4. Return to Abaj TV. Access refreshes automatically after pairing.
5. Use D-pad arrows to navigate. OK opens the selected channel or player controls. Back returns to the channel list.
